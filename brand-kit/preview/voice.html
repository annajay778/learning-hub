Voice & Copy

Campminder is a Hero brand. The camp is the protagonist; we are the trusted ally. Every sentence should pass that test.

You make camp better.
Campminder helps you do it.
The story, in two lines

The five dials

Confident

Not boastful. Show pace of innovation; let clients do the bragging.

Warm

Not sentimental. We love camp; we don't manufacture nostalgia.

Precise

Not jargony. "Applications, payments, staffing" beats "end-to-end platform."

Aspirational

Not performative. Promise outcomes, not effort.

Spacious

Short sentences. Real paragraphs. White space = breathing room.

Never do this

  • No pain-point marketingCamps live their pain every day. Echoing it back creates fatigue and reminds them how long we took to solve it.
  • No therapist voiceEmpathetic tone is fine. Performatively sympathetic isn't.
  • No unverifiable statsIf a number is on the page, it must be defensible. See: "5X investment."
  • No generic vendor lines"Software that makes your camp the best it can be" is Campwise-grade. Anything a competitor could say, they will.
  • No product-at-center sentencesIf the sentence starts with "Campminder," rewrite it.
  • No bullet-list featuresA hero brand uses language, not icon rows.
