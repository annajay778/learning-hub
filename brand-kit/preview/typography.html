Weights in rotation

Light 300 · Regular 400 · Medium 500 · Semibold 600 · Bold 700. The deck leans on 400 for body and 600 for display. Bold 700 is reserved for the tagline and any eyebrow microtype.

Fallback stack

'Host Grotesk', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif. Helvetica Neue is embedded alongside Host Grotesk in the source deck and is the authorized system fallback.

Tracking rules

Display and headings use -0.02em (tight, like the wordmark). Eyebrows and the tagline use 0.22em uppercase — the same spacing as the "UNPACK POSSIBLE" lockup. Body copy stays at 0.
