Rules

  • DoPlace diagonally — top-left + bottom-right, or top-right + bottom-left. Never all four corners.
  • Don'tRotate or skew the brackets. They are orthogonal. Always.
  • DoMaintain 3px stroke at marketing scale. Never scale below 2px.
  • Don'tTint them gray, teal, or anything other than brand purple (or white on dark).
  • DoUse brackets to mark spotlight moments — a hero quote, a key stat, a featured image.
  • Don'tWrap every card on a page in brackets. They are a signature, not a border style.
