The bracket motif

The four corner brackets surrounding the wordmark are a reusable framing device — a viewfinder for the camp moment. Use it to frame any single focal element, never as decoration.

cm

Clearspace

Minimum clearspace around the wordmark is equal to the height of the lowercase "a" (x-height). No other mark, image edge, or text may enter the bracket frame.
