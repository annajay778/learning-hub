Typography

A two-face system. Spezia Wide Medium for display — wide, assured, a confident grotesque built for headlines. TWK Lausanne for paragraphs — a clean Swiss neo-grotesque that holds text at long length without calling attention to itself.

Licensed foundry fonts Spezia Wide is licensed from Luzi Type; TWK Lausanne from Weltkern. Install both for production artifacts. Previews below fall back to Chivo (display) and Inter (body) via Google Fonts — the closest free matches for quick review in this environment.

Rules

  • Two faces, one role each. Spezia Wide is for display only. TWK Lausanne is for body only. Never swap them.
  • Medium is the display weight. Not Regular, not Bold. The "Wide Medium" cut is the system's voice.
  • No italics for emphasis. Use weight, color, or size.
  • Tracking is negative on display (−0.005 to −0.02em), neutral on body.
  • The 0.22em tagline tracking is reserved for UNPACK POSSIBLE treatments — not decoration for regular headings.
