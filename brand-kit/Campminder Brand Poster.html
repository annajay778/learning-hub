04 · The bracket motif

The film-frame brackets wrap both the logo and the monogram — Campminder's signature container. Use diagonally (TL + BR, or TR + BL), 3px stroke, always purple. Reserve for spotlight moments: a hero quote, a featured image, a key stat. Never a border style.

06 · Never

  • Pain-point openers. Camps live their pain daily.
  • Performative empathy. No therapist voice.
  • Unverifiable stats. Sources or silence.
  • Generic vendor lines competitors could copy.
  • Sentences that start with "Campminder."
  • Icon rows in place of real language.
  • Gradients, drop shadows, pill buttons.
